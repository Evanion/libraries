### ⚠️  Breaking Changes

- **feature:** hydrate a provider from a decision set  ([5805cf96](https://github.com/Evanion/libraries/commit/5805cf96))
  `decisions` on `FeatureProvider` and on a bound provider
  takes `DecisionSet<S>` and no longer takes a bare `Decisions<S>` or its
  readonly form. `<FeatureProvider decisions={features.resolve(ctx)}>` is a
  compile error; pass `features.snapshot(ctx)`, which carries the config
  version, the instant and the origin the provider reads.
